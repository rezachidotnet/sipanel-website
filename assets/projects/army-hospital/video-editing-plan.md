## 4. Duration — 33 Seconds

The 33-second duration gives the completion sequence (Shots 6-8) the breathing room it deserves:

- **8 narrative beats.** Construction shots (S1-S5) stay at 3.5s each for urgency. Completion shots (S6-S8) are extended to 4.5s, 5.0s, and 6.0s respectively — slowing the pace as the payoff lands.
- Tells the **complete journey**: excavation → structure → building shape → panels on roof → interior partitions → finished interior → completed entrance → final hero aerial.
- The pacing shift from fast construction cuts to slower completion holds mirrors the project itself: intense build, then the calm of a finished hospital.
- Keeps file size under 14 MB at CRF 23.

## 8. Transition & Edit Rules

- **Cut style:** Crossfade, 0.3s duration between all shots.
- **Shot 1 fade-in:** 0.5s fade from black (premium opening).
- **Shot 8 fade-out:** 0.5s fade to dim at end (clean loop back to Shot 1).
- **Speed adjustments:**
  - S1: 1.0x (natural drone pace, trimmed to 3.5s)
  - S2: 1.6x (compresses 8s crane action into 3.5s — adds urgency)
  - S3: 1.6x (compresses 8s golden-hour orbit into 3.5s)
  - S4: 3.0x (compresses 15s overhead panel installation into 3.5s — timelapse energy)
  - S5: 2.0x (compresses 10s interior work into 3.5s)
  - S6: 2.5x (compresses 15s corridor walk into 4.5s — walkthrough with time to register)
  - S7: 0.8x (slight slow-motion, ground-level entrance — gravitas, 5.0s)
  - S8: 1.0x (natural drone pace, held for 6.0s — the payoff moment, longest shot)
- **No heavy effects:** No zoom, blur, or color grading. Clean cuts and crossfades only.

## 9. Loop Design

- **Shot 1** (aerial construction site) and **Shot 8** (aerial completed hospital) are both drone shots at similar altitudes with the same mountain backdrop. Different era, matching visual language.
- The 0.5s fade at end of S8 and 0.5s fade at start of S1 create a natural breathing pause.
- Narrative loop: "raw earth → finished hospital → raw earth → finished hospital" — the transformation story reinforces on every cycle.
- S7 (ground-level entrance) immediately before S8 (aerial) creates a satisfying ground-to-air transition within the completion sequence.
